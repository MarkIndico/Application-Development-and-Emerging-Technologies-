
# My Favorite Movie: Spider-Man: No Way Home

## About the Movie
**Spider-Man: No Way Home** is a superhero movie about Peter Parker. His identity becomes public, and he asks Doctor Strange for help. However, the spell goes wrong and brings visitors from other universes.

## Movie Information
- **Title:** Spider-Man: No Way Home
- **Genre:** Action, Adventure, Science Fiction
- **Release Year:** 2021
- **Main Character:** Peter Parker
- **Director:** Jon Watts

## Why I Like This Movie
I like this movie because it has exciting action scenes and an interesting story. I also enjoy seeing different versions of Spider-Man work together. The movie teaches us about responsibility, friendship, and helping others.
